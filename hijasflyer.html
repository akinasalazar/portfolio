Las Hijas De La Madre Flyer

Las Hijas De La Madre are a queer Chicanx feminist punk band that came to the University of San Francisco to hold a concert. Being familiar with punk music and playing in a band myself, I understand the grungy crunch that punk concerts provide, so I emulated that through the flyer design. Using the band's color scheme I created spikes of red and highlighted their clothing to be red and black. I also used a typeface that was reminiscent of grunge culture and added hand-drawn elements in the background.
