Rehash Poster

Rehash is a rock band from Florida. This poster was created for their show in San Francisco in early 2026. Rehash is on my list of my all time favorite bands, and I strived to capture the branding of their newest singles into one poster. Their newest single features grassy backgrounds, and an overall light hearted, youthful tone. In a simple yet compositional way, I made their logo match the grass on the bottom to create contrast within the piece. I also added a grain effect to tie elements of the piece together such as the picture of the band and the background.
