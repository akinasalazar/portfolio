Music Showcase Flyer

The music showcase is a bi-yearly event that features student music at the University of San Francisco. For this poster design, I wanted it to really pop out to the viewer, so I encased the title in boxes to make it pop against a dark purple background. I highlighted students from past showcases and encased them in a white outline to also make them pop against the background. This flyer maintains a fun energy while also being eye-catching to the everyday student.
