Dopamine Chamber Poster

This poster was made for the release of Fontaines D.C’s newest album: Dopamine Chamber. Fontaines D.C. is my all time favorite band, so I created this poster out of pure passion when the album got announced. I wanted to capture the color scheme of the album while also maintaining the branding that they provided in teaser posts. The teaser posts included pictures of planets, eyes, and space. I was able to effectively convey the feeling of the album through gaining inspiration from the teaser posts and I was able to add my own spin on it with the glowing birds.
