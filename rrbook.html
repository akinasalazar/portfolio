Robin Roche Book

Robin Roche is a French Graphic Designer and Illustrator. For this project I was tasked with creating an informational book about an artist. Roche caught my eye due to his unique style of graphic design that meshed digital elements and hand-drawn elements into one unified poster. In the booklet I strived to match this style by including hand-drawn elements such as the opening spread with various doodles relating to Roche’s life. Additionally, his style is very similar to my design style, so I was able to let my unique style come through while also trying to match his. Both styles are shown through the assets used, such as textures, chains, barbed wire, and faded drawings.
