Faculty Recital Flyer

The PASJ Presents faculty recital was an event held at University of San Francisco in which music faculty were able to perform various songs. This event was more on the professional side, so I maintained that feeling in the poster design through a structured grid layout and light-hearted colors. My style was able to come through, as I added texture to the poster and stars in the background of the faculty's names.
