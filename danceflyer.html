Gleeson Plaza Dance Showing

The Gleeson Plaza Dance Showing flyer highlights an event held at the University of San Francisco for students engaging in dance classes. In this flyer I strived to balance readability, movement, and a design that was eye-catching. Using a large bold font, viewers can see the flyer from afar. Additionally, I was able to duplicate the figure and put her in a sequence that mimicked a dance.
