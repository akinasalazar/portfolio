Into the Spider-verse Poster

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse is one of my favorite movies, so I created a movie poster in honor. I knew I wanted it to stand out and be minimalistic, but still eye-catching, and Adobe Illustrator was the perfect application to do so. Before creating the poster I rewatched the movie and drafted color schemes and several different compositions. I landed on an action shot of Miles Morales flying through the city from a bottom view. The contrast of the red background against the purple buildings add a nice contrast and the lines of the building draw the eye towards the middle focal point: Spider-Man.
