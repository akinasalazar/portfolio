About Me

Hi, I'm Akina Salazar! I'm a designer based in San Francisco, originally from Santa Clara, California. I'm currently studying design at the University of San Francisco, with a minor in UX/UI (User Experience and User Interface). I've been designing my whole life whether that was drawing Pokémon as a kid, or creating posters for my favorite bands in the present. Music is one of my main inspirations for designs. Some of my favorite artists include Fontaines D.C., Arctic Monkeys, and Rehash. When I'm not designing you can find me playing my guitar, drawing, or skating. I'm open to freelance design work so please feel free to reach out if you would like to work with me!
