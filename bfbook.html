Boneface Tribute Magazine

Boneface is an illustrator from the United Kingdom who specializes in art with defined subjects and distinct colors. This magazine feature is all about Boneface's background, inspiration, works, and design technique, created in a similar style of his art. On the back page I made my own tribute piece that included aspects of his design technique such as utilizing a bold background color, and a heroic featured character.
