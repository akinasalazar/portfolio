Transparency Now! SJSU Flyer

I created this flyer for an organization at San Jose State University that was protesting a program that was funded by Lockheed Martin, a security contractor that was funding the ongoing genocide in Palestine. It was important to me that I was able to design for a cause I believe in, because I love to support student organizations that can make an impact. Especially when it comes to design, I was able to use bold text and the colors of the Palestine flag to catch the attention of any students walking by.
